About Inkspire

Inkspire is an art club at Diamond Bar High School that gives students opportunities to earn volunteer hours while using their creativity.

Members will complete art projects that are donated to local libraries and community organizations.

Our projects may include coloring pages and books, stickers, bookmarks, step-by-step drawing guides, posters, murals, bracelets, keychains, and other creative crafts.

Throughout the process, our officers will provide instructions, feedback, and support to help members complete their projects.
